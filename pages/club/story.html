OUR STORY

From a small boutique gym in 2005 to the family we are today.

2005

The Beginning

BASE BODY FITNESS started in Unit E in Cheadle, Staffordshire in 2005. The vision was simple: create a friendly boutique gym where members are treated like family.

2007

Expansion Begins

Due to rapid growth, additional facilities were added including a dedicated Spinning Centre and Fitness Class Studio.

2008

Park Hall Opens

On the 10th of August 2008, our new Health Club opened in Park Hall, Longton.

Best Customer Service Award

On 30th July 2009 we won the Best Customer Service Award at the Signal Radio Style Awards.

2013

Weights Hall Expansion

To compete in an increasingly crowded fitness market, we expanded into a neighbouring unit and reopened with over 20,000 square feet of facilities.

2020

Pandemic & Rebuild

During lockdowns we were forced to close for 256 days. Thanks to our incredible members we survived and used the time to improve the club.

The Arnold Mural

In 2020 we commissioned a giant wall mural inspired by Arnold Schwarzenegger for our Weights Hall.
