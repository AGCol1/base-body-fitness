PARKINSON'S CLUB

Fitness is for everyone.

More Than Exercise

Our Parkinson's Club is a friendly and welcoming fitness session designed specifically for people living with Parkinson's.

We focus on movement, confidence, wellbeing and friendship. Every session combines exercise, social interaction and support.

After every session, stay for a cuppa and a chat with people who understand the challenges and victories that come with Parkinson's.

Why Exercise Matters

Research suggests that increasing exercise to around 2.5 hours per week can be one of the most effective ways to help manage Parkinson's symptoms.

Exercise can help improve posture, balance, flexibility, endurance and overall quality of life.

Our sessions are suitable for all fitness levels and abilities.
